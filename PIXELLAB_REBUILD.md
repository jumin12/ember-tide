# Integrated V15 build

`index.html` embeds the renderer, game code, all selected art and all selected audio. Edit runtime modules under `elyndor`; rebuild with `python elyndor/tools/build.py --final`. The native packer rejects clipped source frames and records its selected sources in the asset manifest. `release.json` identifies the exact tested artifact.

The V15 modules are `relief-data-v15.js`, `geography-v15.js`, `settlements-v15.js`, `travel-v15.js`, `combat-input-v15.js`, `weather-v15.js`, `clearance-v15.js`, `audio-v15.js` and `casting-v15.js`, with `experience-v15.css`. They extend the established story, traversal, combat and audio systems.

The atlas uses the same overworld height function, roads and river. A small separate Three.js scene shares the existing WebGL renderer; a 2D canvas presents its viewport inside the menu without creating a second WebGL context. It renders at a bounded cadence, supports mouse/keyboard controls and stores a 256Ã—144 exploration field in journey records. Discovered landmarks can be tracked or fast-traveled to; hidden landmarks stay absent. Local maps retain actual floor exits and inn-circle positions.

All saves, including automatic checkpoints, are guarded by the actual inn interior identity. Outdoor save circles are removed. Existing save migrations remain supported. Monster dissolution uses five preallocated 256-fragment batches, with native sprite colors, stable pixel thresholds and fading grounded shadows. Spell/attack origins follow moving actors, and art swaps keep their emissive and shadow maps aligned.

Likely encounter arenas are searched during destination loading. Flat staging is preferred, collision clearance is shared with navigation, and likely side/reflected views are uploaded before controls return. The world keeps visible foliage while using instancing and distance geometry budgets. The river bed remains continuous beneath hills; bridge approaches share the same terrain function.

`regression-v15.py` runs one GPU browser at a time. Its checks cover every location/interior, movement and collision, inn saves, exploration fog and atlas input, the regional story, events, combat/native poses/abilities, recruitment, water systems, menus, portraits/loading, official keyboard smoke, lighting response, decoded audio and performance. Screenshots must also be opened for visual review. The public checkpoint helper lists explicit paths and audits staged bytes; private references and intermediate audio never belong in that list.

V15 derives coast outlines and regional relief from the user-linked Elyndor atlas. Geographic nodes are independent of full-size local floor plans, so Brookstead remains intact. The macro terrain is a cached two-metre triangular field shared by feet, props and collision queries. Roads blend in the terrain shader; river strips follow descending channel levels. Rain uses bounded, seeded world chunks, while cloud samples and their projected shadows use the same world coordinates and clock. Small props keep native texture resolution; surface pages are newly generated at 512 pixels rather than enlarged from old sources.

V17 integrates the same selected character and material bank into a wider geographic world and an atlas captured from its actual meshes. The starting bridge and approaches share the terrain paving shader. Four town stair footprints are cut precisely, retaining ground outside their boundaries. Cutaways use structural mesh intersections. The opening river now has a persistent downstream recovery state, with shared cascade endpoints and reflection heights. Tutorial lessons add 23 recorded world-voice instructions, UI highlights and projected targets; battle status uses opposing upper corners and a persistent enemy arrow. No additional generated character or texture sheets are introduced in this revision.


V18 modules: atlas-data-v18.js, relief-data-v18.js, atlas-v18.js, lesson-v18.js, hydraulics-v18.js, world-v18.js and visibility-v18.js, plus experience-v18.css. Geography is traced from the current atlas and its published volcanic revision; concept paintings are not pasted over gameplay. First-combat practice has a finite sequence and records completion in existing inn saves. Legacy regression fixtures explicitly opt out; revision-v18.mjs exercises the real default lesson through mouse/keyboard commands and measures all moving cascade joins, target/HP/MP/item changes, combat obstruction restoration and canonical atlas positions.


V19 adds ground-v19.js, current-v19.js, title-v19.js and title-v19.css. The title director freezes legacy Brookstead-only cycling, chooses from a shuffled POI bag, and hides party/story-cast meshes without changing saved membership. Save deletion removes only the chosen manual slot’s current and legacy keys. The 27-check suite includes the new title/record/stair/current interaction audit and the existing coached-battle regression.

River trails use a static instanced seed buffer with GPU vertex advection. Loading warms the hidden current and sluice spray, avoiding first-release shader compilation and per-frame terrain sampling.
