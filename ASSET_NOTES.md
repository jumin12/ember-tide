# Elyndor current asset notes

The single offline `index.html` contains 1,565 packed whole poses across 25 families. Twelve roaming families have newly generated individual frames: Bren, Soren, Tavin, Mira, merchant, worker, healer, guard, courier, farmer, elder and innkeeper. Each has 64 walk poses and eight neutral views, totaling 864 new poses. Every generation call produced one complete pose, not a sheet. Full prompts, original images, phase guides, rejected candidates and explicit direction/phase selection remain available under `design/animation-v5` and `elyndor/assets/source/individual-v5`.

The innkeeper is the character completed for this user-requested checkpoint. Lysa, the representative and the two Fern resident families still use their earlier walking art; their new neutral candidates are staged. Combat and other fallback clips remain from the reviewed compact/motion-v4 sources. Running changes gait cadence; it does not yet have a separate authored running set. Lysa's compact plum combat outfit is selected; her legacy roaming set still needs replacement.

Packing is technical: remove disconnected alpha haze, retain the complete silhouette and props, register the whole body's root, uniformly resize onto the common 68-pixel human body unit, then pack losslessly. It does not synthesize legs, warp clothing or mirror body halves. Source poses are preserved. The current pixel audit passes all 1,565 cells and reports zero crop warnings. This establishes packing integrity, not perfect generated anatomy.

The latest fifteen separately generated material tiles are preserved in the development workspace under `elyndor/assets/source/materials-v6`. They supply cobble, timber, slate, plaster, grass, rock, earth, masonry, bark, cloth, metal, dark wood, broadleaf canopy, conifer canopy and crystal facets. Each was produced as one material image with the built-in OpenAI image generator, using the approved environmental concept and compact character as references. Complete prompts, reference paths and original-output provenance are preserved in a JSON beside each PNG. The older v5 material sources remain intact. The new 128-pixel logical texture grid doubles linear detail, then uses nearest-neighbor storage enlargement to 512 pixels. Mipmaps and bounded anisotropic filtering improve angled and distant surfaces without extra geometry passes. Real 3D geometry and shaders supply terrain, planting movement, water, cutaways, lighting and battle effects.

Eighteen approved detailed Drive portraits under `design/drive/portraits-oct26` override provisional main/story portraits. Eight separately generated resident portraits under `elyndor/assets/source/portraits-v5` match their role identities; each has its own prompt/provenance record. HUD face crops are packed separately from the full conversation artwork. Current source documents and eleven illustrated references are preserved read-only under `design/drive`; no Drive document was modified.

The artwork interprets the approved reference designs for this browser renderer. Unspecified dialogue, incidental villagers, numeric tuning and practical prop construction remain implementation drafts. Four walking sets, further idle/running animation, software performance, scenery refinement and broader game regression checks remain. The requested review checkpoint is documented in `output/elyndor/review-v5/CHECKPOINT.md` and its JSON evidence; historical asset notes are archived under `archive/elyndor-before-individual-frames`.

The published game embeds the new material pixels directly in `index.html`. Its four-edge diorama post-processing leaves the central play area and CSS UI sharp; square, wide and portrait GPU probes verify the edge blur. `release.json` identifies the deployed artifact and checks. The development workspace can rebuild it with `python elyndor/tools/build.py --final`. The build reads the current curated sources and does not require legacy Ember & Tide art. Original approved artwork and previous standalone releases remain preserved.
