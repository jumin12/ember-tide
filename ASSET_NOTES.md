# Current asset provenance

All character, portrait, surface, botanical, prop and item artwork selected by the current runtime manifest was created with PixelLab. References were the approved original Google Drive character/scene concepts and mockups, plus the user-provided town concept. Current-pass PixelLab outputs carry the shared visual style forward. Earlier generated game artwork and the superseded text-only prototype are excluded from the build.

The cast contains 41 native design families plus a runtime alias for Soren facing the opposing side. Every roaming family has eight native walking and idle directions; the five party characters have separate eight-direction running cycles. Combat-only ascended counterparts are used only for Bren and Tavin. Townspeople and livestock have no combat requirement. Each regional resident has a distinct appearance.

Frames remain whole native PixelLab poses. Packing centers different native canvas sizes on a shared root baseline and assigns a consistent body unit; it does not slice limbs, mirror combat poses, stretch individual frames or invent motion in the renderer. Lossless WebP embeds the selected pixels in the standalone file. Source and runtime RGBA audits verify registration, transparent gutters and pixel preservation. These checks establish packaging integrity; visual review is still required for generated anatomy and motion.

Reviewed repairs include stable crescent-staff poses, a drawn-sword combat loop, back-apron continuity, removal of baked ground blobs, corrected viewing directions and complete wolf tails. PixelLab's exact editing tools preserve unaffected body pixels. Clipped dialogue portrait hair was repaired on padded transparent canvases; unmasked face and outfit pixels remain identical. The five party HUD portraits are separately generated 128×128 transparent head portraits referenced to the approved character concepts. Other compact speaker portraits derive from the dialogue art. Mira and Lysa share their reviewed physical casting gesture between regular magic and abilities; PixelLab deduplicated those identical requests. Each spell still has its own 3D effects and mechanics.

Fifteen opaque PixelLab material tiles, thirteen prop/botanical images, nine item icons, a UI frame and parchment supply the world interface. Material enlargement uses nearest-neighbor sampling; mipmaps and bounded anisotropic filtering handle angled surfaces. Geometry supplies dimensional branches, grass, flowers, walls, roofs, terrain terraces, stairs, bridges and furniture. Shader animation supplies wind, reactive planting, water, atmosphere, lighting and battle effects.

The layout is an authored 3D interpretation of the town concept. Terrain height, visible stairs, collision and navigation share coordinates. Rivers continue through the connected landscape, with separate heights and reflection targets for the raised pools. No concept image is used as a flat gameplay background.

Private source concepts, upload receipts, signed URLs and account credentials are not part of the published project. Historical artwork may remain in repository history, but the current manifest and standalone artifact contain only the selected new pass. Native sources and the public selection are under `elyndor/assets/source/pixellab-v7`.

The waterworks strength action uses eight additional native PixelLab Bren poses, reviewed and registered without limb slicing. This gesture keeps his sword sheathed. Melee strikes, bow releases and spell gestures are selected separately; ranged attacks remain planted and projectiles arrive before damage resolves.

The ground revision adds accepted 128×128 native grass and earth swatches in the same palette. Earlier scene-like candidates were rejected and are absent from the manifest. Main roads consistently use paving; separate trails blend into earth.

The world-design revision adds a 128×128 granite albedo swatch without illustrated boulder faces. Rounded 3D rock geometry supplies the shape, with bank stones embedded in the actual terrain. Bren and his Ascended form have new native left-profile combat clips. Their three middle sword poses were individually corrected in PixelLab after rejecting backwards swings; contact is the fifth pose. Reviewed recoil selection omits an Ascended turn away from the attacker. Native registration and lossless packing apply to these frames too.
