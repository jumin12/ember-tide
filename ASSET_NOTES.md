# Selected artwork and recorded audio

The approved Google Drive character, portrait, environment and storyboard concepts guide the art. Only approved concepts were sent to PixelLab as references for this update. Earlier generated game assets were not used as reference inputs. Private source documents, uploaded-reference receipts, credentials, auditions and rejected candidates are excluded from the public checkpoint.

The runtime retains 41 selected native families plus Soren's explicitly authored opponent direction, 4,878 reviewed native poses, 38 dialogue portraits and five dedicated HUD portraits. Source margins and packed RGBA pixels are audited. The approved Duskfang Wolf has charcoal-blue fur, ivory brow horns and a quill mane; the Briartusk Boar has rusty umber fur, natural bark-like armor and thorn-like tusks. Their defeat fragments sample actual sprite colors.

V14 adds six 256×256 PixelLab material swatches: pale river cobbles, old mossy paving, irregular limestone masonry, warm lime plaster, forest soil and meadow ground. Architectural material variants retain their layer, color, emission and physical UVs through merging. Terrain samples use stable world coordinates and blended phases rather than changing random offsets over time.

Four 688×384 PixelLab loading illustrations were generated from approved Drive concepts: Bren overlooking the river town, Soren studying by an inn window, a hearth/reading lounge, and an ancient Elder Light remnant. Their lossless WebP files preserve the native pixel grid. Loading text describes existing characters and story elements; the pictures are illustrations rather than gameplay screenshots.

ElevenLabs generated all 248 selected dialogue and battle clips using `eleven_v3`. Five original voices were designed for the young-adult main cast, with restrained emotional direction for concern, resolve and quieter moments. Supporting casting follows the selected portraits, including female merchants/couriers/quartermasters and younger apprentices. Sixteen casting roles are recorded in the audio catalog. Voice generation requests and auditions remain private.

Thirteen original music cues, ten ambience loops and thirty-five action/foley sounds remain selected from the preceding ElevenLabs sound pass. The 306 total selected recordings use Ogg Opus and are embedded. Decode, duration, amplitude and exact-payload audits cover every recording. The game contains no speech API key and makes no live speech-generation request.

The browser world is an authored 3D interpretation of the approved concepts. The 3D atlas derives its relief and roads from the physical overworld and keeps explored fog in the save data. No concept painting is pasted over gameplay to substitute for a playable level.
